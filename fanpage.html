Bio

Date of Birth: 28 May 1997

Gender: Female

Birthplace: Wuhan, Hubei, China

Wang Yuwen (Chinese: 王玉雯; born 28 May 1997) is a Chinese actress. A graduate of the Beijing Dance Academy, she is acclaimed for her standout performance in the Golden Horse Award-winning film An Elephant Sitting Still (2018), along with hit romantic television series such as Once We Get Married (2021) and The Love You Give Me (2023).

Fun Facts About Wang Yuwen

  1. Before acting, she spent years training in classical and ethnic folk dance at the elite Beijing Dance Academy.
  2. She is a skilled pianist and plays classical pieces during filming breaks.
  3. She frequently carries vintage film cameras on set to capture candid behind-the-scenes moments.
